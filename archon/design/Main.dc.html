What exists today.

Every number below comes from our current test set — one wearer, one room. Making it work on any hand is the mountain we are climbing, in public, with the waitlist watching.

REAL TODAY
A working prototype on a dev board — an Arduino-class chip with a 6-axis motion sensor, worn on a finger strap
Three air gestures recognized: swipe left-to-right, swipe right-to-left, and an air-drawn circle
The neural network runs entirely on the device — it works in airplane mode
A 1.00 F1 score on our current validation set — small, but clean, after hand-auditing every mislabeled sample

How it works.

1.
Sense
A 6-axis motion sensor samples your hand’s acceleration and rotation about a hundred times per second.
2.
Think
A tiny neural network runs on the ring itself and names the gesture in about one millisecond. Nothing leaves your finger.
3.
Act
Bluetooth carries the command to whatever you point it at: next slide, play and pause, lights on.
